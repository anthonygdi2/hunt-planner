# Code of conduct

Hunt Planner is a small fan project for Tibia players. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or reviews) is expected to keep it friendly and useful.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the game, to GitHub or to code.
- Assume good faith. Players come from many countries, and many write in their second language.
- Keep feedback about the work: the code, the numbers, the wording. Say what you’d change and why.
- Back up game numbers with a source (a TibiaWiki page, an in-game screenshot, a video), and accept that the maintainer decides what goes in.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, slurs, or attacks on someone’s identity, nationality, language or skill.
- Sexual content, threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Posting someone else’s private information, including their characters, backups or account details, without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, or selling in-game items, characters or services.
- Requests for help with cheating, botting or anything else against Tibia’s rules.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who don’t follow this code, with or without a warning, depending on how serious it is.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, use GitHub’s **Report content** (the ⋯ menu on any comment, issue or pull request) or [report the account](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) to GitHub. For something you’d rather not post publicly, ask in a new issue for a private way to reach the maintainer, without the details. Reports are handled as privately as possible.

This code applies in this repository’s spaces on GitHub. It was written for this project, in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
