# Improvements: the ranked list

What to build next, in order, with why. Re-evaluate it after each step ships (prompt at the end). Started November 13, 2026.

Value is what it does for the player's question, "where should I hunt, and is it worth my time?". Effort is the size of the change including tests, translations and docs.

Golden output: when a step is meant to change what the app computes, `npm run golden:update` is fine once every difference is checked to come from that change (maintainer, November 13, 2026). Unexpected differences still stop the step and go to the maintainer.

## Done

- **Log every board** (November 13, 2026). Picked and Rerolled in the Bounty editor now log the board when its three creatures are typed, in Basic and Advanced mode alike, as Log this board does (`logOffers` in `app/app.js`, once per board), and the picked offer gets `picked: true` (also on a board logged by hand before it was picked). Partly typed boards aren't logged. Log this board stays for boards not picked or rerolled in the app; the log is still shown only in Advanced mode, and logging doesn't change the odds the model uses. So the logged Silver/Gold shares and the ×5 check (item 9) see every board, not just the remembered ones, and the picks are there for the winter update's per-creature odds (W3). No golden change.
- **Slot lists with each logged board** (November 13, 2026). Log this board also keeps, per offer, whether it was Preferred (`pref`) or Unwanted (`unw`), and the pool and in-pool list sizes then (`nPool`, `nPref`, `nUnw`; `boardSlotTags` in `core/slots.js`), so the unofficial ×5 Preferred weight can be tested once enough boards are logged (item 9). Boards logged before this have none of it. No golden change.
- **Silver/Gold odds for rerolls** (November 13, 2026). The reroll model weighs each future offer as Silver or Gold with the character's own odds (`offerOdds` on the profile), else the guess in `rules.js` (`OFFER_ODDS`: 10% Silver, 2% Gold, the maintainer's). In Advanced mode the Bounty editor has Log this board (each offer's creature, kills, kind and level into `rewardObs`, once per board) and the odds, with the logged shares beside them, Use my logged shares and Use the guess. One set of odds for every difficulty; the log keeps the difficulty. Silver and Gold triple the offers the reroll solver weighs, so `solvePlan` now jumps ahead every 10 sweeps and stops on an error bound: as fast as before with three times the offers, and closer to its fixed point (the old stop was about 3e-4 short). Golden output updated (reroll values and thresholds).
- **Prey loot in profit** (November 13, 2026). A loot Prey raises the profit of every spot its creature lives at: the spot's loot/h × the creature's share of it (by exp, an estimate) × the bonus. Reference entries carry `lootph`; own hunts use their logged loot. Details in `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` (How the numbers work, The plan).

## Next, in order

Re-evaluated seven times on November 13, 2026, last after the slot lists were recorded with each logged board, valued by how the maintainer uses the tool: reference hunts only show that a spot exists; a test lap logs their own rates before they pick bounties. So what counts is picking bounties, rerolling, and accurate values from the player's own logged hunts.

What the last re-evaluation changed (numbers as they are now, after Log every board shipped):

- **Log every board** was new and led: the ×5 check (item 9) needs every board, and Log this board is a click that's easy to skip on dull boards, which biases the shares. It shipped the same day (Done), so the rest moved up one.
- **Bounty Talisman** moved up (1st now). Test laps are logged before a bounty is picked, so they never include the Talisman's loot bonus; for a profit goal, an offer's spots are worth less in the plan than they will be on the bounty.
- **Bonuses during a logged hunt** is new (2nd). A hunt logged with a loot Prey or the boosted creature on keeps their extra loot in its profit for good, and then a loot Prey set today is counted again on top. Raw exp is already free of bonuses; loot isn't.
- **Bestiary kills** (was 1st) moved to 3rd: it matters for charm goals only, and the maintainer's priorities are bounty choice and accurate own numbers. Its design is unchanged.
- **Boosted creature's double loot** (4th) shares its open rule with item 2: build it with or right after that.
- **Best use of bounty points** (was 3rd) moved to 6th: High effort, it rests on the ×5 weight (unchecked until the board log has gathered enough), and the winter update changes what skipping and slots are worth (Platinum, W1; per-creature odds, W3), so building it before the update means rebuilding part of it.
- **Prey suggestion** (was 5th) moved to 7th: it doesn't help pick a bounty or reroll.
- The winter update isn't live (checked on tibia.com in Chrome, November 13, 2026): the test server is still private, a public test is promised “later”, no release date. Nothing moves out of “Waiting on the winter update”.
- A Double XP weekend runs between the server saves of November 20 and 23, 2026 (tibia.com news, November 13). The Today's boosts exp event already counts it, on hunting exp only. Worth checking in game that weekend whether a bounty task's reward exp doubles too; if it does, the event should also raise the task reward share in `spotValue`.

## Considered, not planned

- Comparing a logged hunt with the reference (was 1st, moved November 13, 2026): “82% of the reference exp/h here”, on the log preview and each hunt on the spot page. The maintainer uses reference hunts to learn that a spot exists, does a test lap and logs their own rates before picking bounties, so the comparison doesn't change their choices. The design stays in git history (`docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md` before this move) if players ask for it.
- Prey damage and defense, and the boosted creature's faster respawn: they change kill rates, which the plan takes from hunts, not from rules.
- The Talisman's damage and life leech (maintainer, November 13, 2026: ignore for now). Damage would raise the kill rate on the bounty creature and life leech would lower supplies, but there's no rule for how much. They're entered (item 1) so the advisor can show their prices, but not valued.
- Weekly "time saved" model, hunting task points, soulseals, delivery tasks: no one has asked.
- Duo and team hunts (was 7th, moved November 13, 2026): no reference entry is duo or team (all 733 are solo; the duo showcases sit under Maybe in `docs/REFERENCE_VIDEOS.md`, unread), so it needs readings, a way to split a team's exp and loot, and a UI, and no one has asked.
- Prey lasting 2 hours of hunting: with a session length over 2 hours the plan counts an exp or loot Prey for the whole session. Small, and players who renew Prey with wildcards get it the whole time anyway; revisit if longer sessions with Prey look overvalued.

## Re-evaluating the list

After each step ships, a new session gets this prompt:

> Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` and `docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md` in this repo. The last step built is the newest entry under Done in `docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md`; look at how it was built (`git log`, the code it names). Then re-evaluate the list under “Next, in order”: check each item against the code as it is now (is it still needed, did the last step make it cheaper or change its design, is its value or effort wrong), add anything the last step exposed, drop or move to “Considered, not planned” what no longer earns its place, and re-order. Value items by how the maintainer uses the tool: reference hunts only show that a spot exists; they do a test lap and log their own rates before picking bounties. So comparing hunts with the reference matters little; picking bounties, rerolling and accurate values from their own logged hunts matter most. Check whether the winter update is live yet (tibia.com news; direct fetches and the in-app browser hit a bot check, so use Claude in Chrome) and move what it unblocked into the list. Update `docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md` with your changes. Then tell me which item to build next and why, and what decisions it needs from me before you start. Don't build anything yet.
