# Improvements: the ranked list

What to build next, in order, with why. Re-evaluate it after each step ships (prompt at the end). Started November 13, 2026.

Value is what it does for the player's question, "where should I hunt, and is it worth my time?". Effort is the size of the change including tests, translations and docs.

Golden output: when a step is meant to change what the app computes, `npm run golden:update` is fine once every difference is checked to come from that change (maintainer, November 13, 2026). Unexpected differences still stop the step and go to the maintainer.

## The Talisman's damage and life leech

Looked into on November 15, 2026. Nothing official says how either is applied; what follows is community reports and reasoning, so both rest on assumptions that need the maintainer's OK, as the loot stacking did.

**What's known**

- The four paths are damage, life leech, more loot and double bestiary; life leech replaced the Bounty Ring's damage reduction when it became the Talisman (a Tibia news mirror on tibiaroute.com). There's no mana leech path. All apply to the bounty creature only.
- Sizes (TibiaQA, community-reported): damage and life leech start at 2.5% and cap at 50%. Steps are +0.5% a level to 10%, then +0.25% to 20%, then +0.1% (players' reports, December 2025 to March 2026). The price formula 12n − 7 is disputed by its own author (March 2026: after 10% the price is said to rise by 2 a level, not 12). The top bounty score was about 82,000 points after five months (April 2026), so real Talismans sit near 10% to 20% on a favoured path, not 50%.
- Not published: whether the damage bonus multiplies with or adds to Prey, charms and imbuements; whether the life leech adds to Vampirism.
- Life leech on several creatures: a 2020 TibiaQA test with 25% leech and area runes healed 25%, 14%, 10.1% and 8.2% of the damage per creature on 1 to 4 Dragon Lords. That fits leech % × (0.1 + 0.9 ÷ n) per creature for n creatures hit (25, 13.75, 10, 8.1), so the whole heal is leech % × the damage on one creature × (0.9 + 0.1 × n). The fit is this document's, from that one test; no source states the formula. Unknown: how it works when only one of the creatures hit carries the leech, as the Talisman's does.
- The Hunting Session Analyser text has Damage, Damage/h, Healing and Healing/h lines. The app keeps both totals with each hunt since November 15, 2026 (`core/session.js`); nothing reads them yet. Whether Healing counts leeched hit points wasn't found; one hunt with and one without leech at the same spot would show it.

**Damage: how to count it**

- A hunt's time is killing plus everything else (walking, luring, waiting for respawn). Only the killing gets shorter, and only the part of it spent on the bounty creature. So kill rate × 1 ÷ (1 − f × s × d ÷ (1 + d)), with f the share of time spent killing, s the bounty creature's share of the hit points killed, d the bonus.
- The most it can be is with f = 1: 10% damage on a creature that is half the spot is +4.8%; on a creature that is all of it, +10%. With f = 0.5 those are +2.3% and +4.8%.
- The maintainer's two cases are both in it. Overkill (the creature dies in one turn with or without the bonus) and a spot where respawn is the limit are both f = 0: no gain. A creature that is a small share of the kills has a small s.
- Rough spots: with area attacks a pack is done when its toughest creature dies, so a bonus on a creature that isn't the last to die saves less than its hit-point share says, and on the toughest one more. Turns are whole numbers, so a small bonus sometimes saves no turn at all; over a hunt this averages out unless creatures die in one or two turns.
- f can't be read from one hunt (the Damage line is about the hit points killed, whatever the pace). It can be measured: the kill rate of hunts on the bounty against hunts without, at the same spot, now that the bonus is recorded with each hunt. Until then it is 0.5, a guess (`KILL_TIME`), and 0 at a spot switched to respawn-limited.
- Built on November 15, 2026 (Done) with the creature's exp share standing in for its hit-point share. Still open: hit points per creature from TibiaWiki, and replacing the guess for f with the measurement (both under Loot value per creature's data work, or their own item once there are enough recorded hunts).

**Life leech: how to count it**

- Healing per hour = leech % × kills/h × hit points of the bounty creature × the area factor. At 10%, 400 kills/h of an 8,000 hit point creature and area attacks on six creatures (factor 0.25): 80,000 hit points an hour, about 56,000 gold for a knight on health potions. On a single target the factor is 1 and the heal would be four times that, more than most hunts need, so it must be capped at the healing actually done (the Healing line).
- What a healed hit point costs differs by vocation and is the weakest number: knights buy health potions (a supreme health potion heals about 875 for 625 gold, about 0.7 gold a hit point; from memory, to check against TibiaWiki when built); the others heal mostly with spells, paid in mana potions, and the spell's healing grows with level and magic level.
- Leech only saves supplies when the character is hurt; healing while at full health is lost. The cap at logged Healing covers the total, not the timing.
- So the honest order is: record first, look at whether supplies per kill fall on bounty hunts, and build the estimate only if they do.

**Counted once**

Hunts on the bounty already carry both bonuses in their kill rate and supplies, and the creature's own numbers prefer those hunts. The gap is the other direction, as it was for loot: a test lap or a reference spot has neither bonus when offers are compared, and a bounty hunt's faster kills are pooled into the spot's rates for every other creature. Recording the percents with the hunt (Done, November 15, 2026) lets the rates take them out and the plan add them once.

Sources: tibiaqa.com questions 38086, 38625, 38062 (Talisman paths and steps), 14483 (life leech on several creatures); tibiaroute.com “Winter Update Changes during Test Server” (ring to Talisman, life leech). TibiaWiki's Life Leech page refused the fetch.

## Considered, not planned

- Comparing a logged hunt with the reference (was 1st, moved November 13, 2026): “82% of the reference exp/h here”, on the log preview and each hunt on the spot page. The maintainer uses reference hunts to learn that a spot exists, does a test lap and logs their own rates before picking bounties, so the comparison doesn't change their choices. The design stays in git history (`docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md` before this move) if players ask for it.
- Prey defense and the boosted creature's faster respawn: they change kill rates and supplies, which the plan takes from hunts, not from rules. (Prey damage is counted since November 15, 2026, with the Talisman's damage.)
- Weekly "time saved" model, hunting task points, soulseals, delivery tasks: no one has asked.
- Duo and team hunts (was 7th, moved November 13, 2026): no reference entry is duo or team (all 733 are solo; the duo showcases sit under Maybe in `docs/REFERENCE_VIDEOS.md`, unread), so it needs readings, a way to split a team's exp and loot, and a UI, and no one has asked.
- Prey lasting 2 hours of hunting: with a session length over 2 hours the plan counts an exp or loot Prey for the whole session. Small, and players who renew Prey with wildcards get it the whole time anyway; revisit if longer sessions with Prey look overvalued.
