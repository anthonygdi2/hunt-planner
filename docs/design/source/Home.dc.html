  1. “Plan is using” chips. Every optional input in one row: goal mix, bounty (the three offers or your active one, plus tokens), weekly, today’s boosts (boosted creature, prey, exp event), session length, data sources. A set chip shows its value. An empty one is dashed and its editor opens with one line on what adding it changes. Replaces both “Options” and the separate “Your board”.
  2. Inline editor. One chip open at a time, expanding in the page, so nothing covers the keyboard. A live “Plan:” line at its foot shows the effect while you type. Done collapses it.
  3. Step 1. The answer, with the two biggest buttons. Free turns the card into “Hunting at Cobra Bastion” with an optional “Log a clean hunt”. No timer, no kill counting. Source badge and Why? live on the card.
  4. Fallbacks. “Taken? →” steps, compact, each with its own Taken. Tapping Taken on step 1 moves the next one up.
  5. End of chain. “Reroll and re-plan” while you have tokens (with an active bounty, a reroll drops it), else “See Spots for more”.
  6. Taken spots. Every spot marked Taken in the last ~45 minutes, with time left. Free again clears it early. Hidden when empty.
  7. Tabs. Plan, Spots, Creatures (the old Classic view), Weekly. On desktop the chips and editors become a left column, the steps a right column.
